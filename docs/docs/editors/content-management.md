# Content Management

This guide covers the everyday tasks you'll perform as a content editor, from creating new content to updating existing material.

## Creating New Content

You can find all your content types on the sidebar, categorized as [Collections](/concepts/collections) or [Singles](/concepts/singles). By navigating to the type of content you would like to edit you will either see a list of all the content under the collection or go straight to editing a single piece of content.

You are only able to create new content under a collection. Editorial uses a WYSIWYG editor that is powered by [Markdown](https://www.markdownguide.org/getting-started/) but you don't need to know anything about it to edit content.

## Building Content with Blocks

Some fields let you compose content from blocks, for example an article made of image and text sections. Use **Add block** to choose the type of block to add at the end of the list.

- Drag a block by its handle to change its position.
- Click a block's title to collapse or expand it. Collapsed blocks show a short summary of their content.
- Use the bin icon to remove a block.

Blocks with errors stay expanded and are outlined in red until the errors are fixed.

## Editing at the Same Time as Someone Else

Only one person can edit an item at a time. When you change something in an item, it is locked for you until you save it, leave the page, or are inactive for 5 minutes.

If someone else is editing an item you open, a message shows who it is and when they were last active, and the item can't be changed or deleted. Once they are done, it reloads with their changes and you can edit it. The same happens if you have the item open in another browser tab.

If you are inactive for 5 minutes with unsaved changes, someone else can start editing the item. If they do, or if they save it, your changes can't be saved anymore: copy anything you want to keep, then use **Reload** to see the latest version.
